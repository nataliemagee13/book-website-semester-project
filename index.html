How Social Media Shapes Reading Trends

A digital culture project about BookTok, Goodreads, genre trends, and how online recommendations change what people read.

My main idea

My project argues that social media shapes reading trends by making certain books, genres, and authors more visible over time. BookTok does not just recommend books. It changes the timeline of popularity by helping older books come back, pushing genres like romantasy and thrillers, and giving women authors more visibility through reader-driven content.

See literature review

Why I Chose This Topic:

I chose this topic because I like reading and I also notice how much social media influences what books people talk about. A lot of the books I hear about now come from TikTok, Goodreads, or online recommendation lists. I wanted to look deeper at how those trends actually happen instead of just assuming books become popular randomly.

About the Creator

Hi, I’m Natalie. I created this website because I wanted to look at how social media shapes what people read, especially through BookTok, Goodreads, and online recommendation culture. I like that this project lets me combine research with something I actually care about, like books, trends, and digital media.

This site is part research project and part personal reading space. I wanted it to feel useful and easy to click through, not just like a long paper online.

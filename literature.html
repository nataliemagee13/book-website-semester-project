What The Research Says about BookTok:

1. BookTok is Not Just a Trend, it is a Recommendation System

A lot of the research I found shows that BookTok is more than people casually talking about books. It works like a recommendation system because readers post reviews, reactions, rankings, and short videos that make certain books more visible. This connects to my project because I am looking at how that visibility turns into larger reading trends.

Dera, 2024 · Low et al., 2023

2. Algorithms Help Decide What Becomes Visible

The research also shows that algorithms matter. Readers may feel like they are just seeing random recommendations, but platforms like TikTok and Goodreads are designed to push some content more than others. This means book popularity is shaped by both readers and the platform itself.

Murray, 2020 · Maity et al., 2018

3. Reading Has Become More Public and Social

Another pattern in the research is that reading is not as private as it used to be. BookTok makes reading more public because people post reactions, reviews, favorite quotes, rankings, and emotional responses. This helps explain why books with strong reactions, plot twists, romance, fantasy worlds, or self-help advice do well online.

Steiner, 2025 · Hyatt, 2024

4. Goodreads Shows How Recommendation Platforms Shape Discovery

Goodreads also matters because it shows how reviews, ratings, shelves, and algorithmic suggestions can influence what people read. This connects to my project because social media is not just TikTok. Reading trends are shaped by a larger online recommendation culture.

Goodreads · Murray, 2020

5. What My Project Adds

My project builds on this research by looking at Google Trends data for BookTok, popular genres, and specific books. Instead of only saying that BookTok is influential, I am trying to show how search interest changes over time and how different kinds of books trend in different ways.

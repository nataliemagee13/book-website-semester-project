How I Researched The Project:

My Research Question:

The goal of my reasearch was to answer the question: How does social media shape reading trends? I wanted to look at this by combining research sources with trend data and examples of books that became popular through online recommendation culture.

Sources I Used:

I used academic sources about BookTok, Goodreads, algorithms, and digital reading culture. I also used industry and platform sources like Barnes & Noble, TikTok, Publishers Weekly, and Goodreads to see how BookTok shows up outside of academic research.

Google Trends Data:

For the data part, I used Google Trends to track search interest over time. I looked at BookTok, popular genres, and specific book titles. I used this because Google Trends helped me see whether online attention changed over time instead of only relying on opinion.

Why I Chose These Books and Genres:

I chose genres like romance, romantasy, fantasy, thrillers, and self-help because they show up often in BookTok recommendations. I also chose books like Atomic Habits, Fourth Wing, The Housemaid, It Ends With Us, and A Court of Thorns and Roses because they show different types of popularity. Some stay popular for years, some spike around a release, and some become popular later through online recommendations.

How I Analyzed The Data

After collecting the Google Trends graphs, I looked for patterns in the timing and shape of each trend. I paid attention to whether interest grew slowly, spiked quickly, stayed high, or came back after a book had already been published. This helped me compare different types of BookTok influence.

Limits of My Method

Google Trends does not prove exactly why someone searched for a book or whether they actually read it. It also cannot separate BookTok from other causes like movie adaptations, publisher marketing, or controversy. Because of that, I used the graphs as evidence of attention over time, not as perfect proof of cause and effect.

How I Used AI Tools

I used Copilot and ChatGPT to help with starter code, page structure, and organizing ideas, but I edited the wording, added my own research, chose the data, and made decisions about what the site should include. Using AI helped me get started technically, but I still had to decide what was actually useful for my project.

How I Built the Website

I built the website using HTML and CSS in VS Code and uploaded it through GitHub. The website is the main deliverable because it presents my research, data, book examples, and findings in a visual way instead of only as a written paper.

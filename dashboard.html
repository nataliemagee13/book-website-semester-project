2. Genres

Genres that are easier to trend online. Looking at genre search trends helped me see that BookTok is not only making individual books popular. It is also making certain kinds of books more visible. Romance, romantasy, fantasy, thrillers, and self-help books all show strong search interest over time.

Source: Google Trends data collected for this project

Romantasy

Romantasy stayed low for years and then started rising quickly around 2023 and 2024.

Romance Books

Romance books grow a lot after 2021 and reach some of their highest points in 2025 and 2026.

Fantasy Books

Fantasy books show steady interest with bigger spikes around 2024 and 2025.

Thriller Books

Thrillers work well online because readers like sharing reactions to plot twists and fast-paced stories.

Self-Help Books

Self-help books rise toward 2025 and 2026 because advice can be turned into quotes and short videos.

Genre takeaway

BookTok is not only pushing specific titles. It is also helping certain genres become more visible. Romantasy is the clearest example because the term rises quickly as fantasy romance books become more popular online.

📚

⏳

Some books spike quickly, some build slowly, and some stay visible for years.

Summary

Overall, this data helps me show that BookTok is not just popular in a general way. Search interest changes over time, and different genres and books follow different patterns. Google Trends does not prove exactly why people read certain books, but it helps show how online attention connects to larger reading trends.

5. Limits of Google Trends

Google Trends is useful because it shows search interest over time, but it does not prove exactly why someone searched for something or whether they actually read the book. A spike could come from BookTok, a movie, a release date, controversy, publisher marketing, or multiple causes at once. I am using the graphs as evidence of visibility and attention, not as perfect proof of cause and effect.

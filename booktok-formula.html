The BookTok Formula

After looking at my Google Trends data and the books that show up again and again online, I noticed that BookTok popularity is not totally random. Certain genres and themes seem to work better because they are easy to explain quickly, easy to react to, and easy to share. This page breaks down the “formula” I noticed behind books that spread online.

Genres That Perform Well Online

The genres that kept showing up in my project were romance, romantasy, fantasy, thrillers, YA, and self-help. These genres work well on social media because they usually give readers something easy to react to, like a plot twist, emotional relationship, fantasy world, favorite trope, or piece of advice.

Romantasy

Romantasy works well online because it combines fantasy worlds with romance, which gives readers a lot to react to and discuss.

Romance

Romance books spread through favorite tropes, emotional moments, character edits, and quick recommendations.

Thrillers

Thrillers are easy to recommend because readers can post reactions to twists without giving away the ending.

YA / Coming-of-Age

YA books connect well to online fan communities, especially when they also connect to shows, movies, or nostalgia.

Self-Help

Self-help books stay visible because their ideas can be turned into short advice posts, quote videos, and lifestyle content.

Themes That Make Books Shareable

The books that do well online usually have themes people can explain or react to quickly. This matters because TikTok is built around short videos, so books with clear emotional hooks or easy talking points have an advantage.

Hashtags Turn Books Into Trends

Hashtags matter because they group books into searchable trends. A hashtag can help readers find similar books, follow a genre, or join a bigger conversation. This is one reason BookTok can turn a single recommendation into a larger trend.

Why This Connects to Sales

BookTok does not just create online attention. It can also connect to book sales and bookstore marketing. Publishers Weekly reported that about 59 million print book sales in 2024 could be tied to BookTok-related influencers or content. TikTok also reported that BookTok-recommended books sold more than 50 million copies across Europe in 2025. This shows that online attention can turn into real-world buying behavior.
